How it works
  • Extract

    An LLM rewrites every abstract as atomic contribution claims: what the paper adds, without background or boilerplate.

  • Map

    Claims are embedded with SPECTER2 and clustered across all years into topics.

  • Trace

    Every rising or falling topic opens down to its claims and the papers behind them.
